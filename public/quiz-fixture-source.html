This authored test specification is not lecture material and is not independently validated educational content.

Label comparisons use literal equality or letter containment as stated. A equals A; Z equals Z. AB, AC, and AD contain A; BB does not. ZX and ZZ contain Z; YY does not.

Practice matching uses identical color labels with no reuse. The cumulative fixture matches AX and BX to their last letter X and explicitly allows reuse.

Ordering uses increasing numeric order: 1, 2, 3 for practice; 4, 5, 6 for the fresh cumulative fixture.

Practice text accepts “blue sky” or “azure”, ignoring case and outer spaces and collapsing inner whitespace. Its numeric answer is 10/4 = 2.50 kg, rounded to 2 decimal places, absolute tolerance 0.01 kg inclusive.

Cumulative text accepts “north” or “N”, ignoring case and outer spaces only. Its numeric answer is 9/2 = 4.5 m, rounded to 1 decimal place, zero tolerance.
